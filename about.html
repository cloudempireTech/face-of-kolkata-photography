Why Couples Choose Our Atelier

Serving Dum Dum, Salt Lake, New Town, Baguiati, Lake Town, Alipore, and Destination venues across Bengal with unwavering creative dedication.

1. Master of Bengali Rituals

Our lead photographers have deep knowledge of Bengali Vedic traditions — anticipating the exact seconds of Bor Boron, Subho Drishti, Saat Paake Ghaura, Kanya Daan, and Sindoor Daan without missing a frame.

2. Custom LUT Color Grading

We never use generic Instagram filters. Every wedding gallery undergoes custom tone-curve balancing calibrated specifically for Indian skin complexions, golden jewellery glints, and deep crimson silks.

3. Guaranteed Delivery Timelines

Receive a 40-frame highlight teaser within 72 hours for your social announcements, full curated photo gallery in 3 weeks, and cinematic feature film in 6–8 weeks.
