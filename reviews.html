Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking wedding dates, deliverables, gear, and album customizations.

How far in advance should we reserve our dates?

Peak Bengali wedding dates (November through March, and May/June) often book 6 to 9 months in advance. We accept a limited number of weddings per auspicious Lagna date to maintain our high artistic standard.

When do we receive the photos and films?

You receive an curated 40-frame highlight teaser within 72 hours for social announcements. Full color-corrected galleries are delivered within 3 weeks, and cinematic 4K films in 6–8 weeks.

Do we get access to the high-resolution files?

Yes, all deliverables are uploaded to a private high-speed cloud gallery with full printing rights and high-resolution master downloads for your family.
