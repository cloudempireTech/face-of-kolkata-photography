Structured Craftsmanship

The 5-Stage Storytelling Journey

A seamless, stress-free creative experience designed to ensure your wedding celebrations unfold naturally while we preserve every emotional milestone.

Stage 01

Discovery & Story Consultation

We meet in person at our Dum Dum studio or over coffee/video call to understand your love story, family traditions, venue lighting, and the unique ritual nuances you cherish most.

  • Detailed ceremony timing alignment
  • Pre-wedding outfit & palette guidance
Stage 02

Shot-List & Crew Choreography

Our directors craft a personalized ritual blueprint — assigning specific lead cinematographers to the bride’s dressing suite, groom's Bor Jatri convoy, and stage mandap setup.

  • Subho Drishti & Sindoor Daan angle mapping
  • Audio mic coordination for priests & vows
Stage 03

Wedding Day Documentary Coverage

On your wedding day, our crew moves like silent observers. We blend naturally into your family circle, capturing pure raw emotions without turning your wedding into an artificial movie set.

  • Simultaneous dual-side coverage (Bride & Groom)
  • Real-time on-site redundant data backup
Stage 04

Fine-Art Grading & 4K Editing

Every photograph is individually processed for true-to-life skin tones and rich Banarasi silk reds. Our cinema editors craft a rhythmic teaser film with customized orchestral scoring.

  • 72-Hour Social Teaser delivery
  • Online private cloud gallery for easy sharing
Stage 05

Archival Heirloom Handover

You select your favorite frames for your handcrafted Italian leatherette album. We print on anti-fade archival photo sheets, flush-mounted in a personalized keepsake presentation case.

  • Layflat panorama viewing experience
  • Lifetime digital master backup storage
